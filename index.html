💬 Have Questions?

We're Here To Help — Answers to the most common questions about our studio, sessions, outfits, and deliverables.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. We recommend booking your session in advance so our photography team can prepare the studio, lighting, and customized props according to your specific requirements.

Do you provide home or doorstep photography?

Currently, Snapilla Studio provides photography sessions exclusively at our dedicated studio. Our controlled studio setup allows us to deliver high-end lighting and aesthetic backdrops that cannot be replicated at home.

Can I bring family or friends?

Yes, absolutely! You are welcome to bring family members or friends depending on the type and package size of your session.

Can I bring my own outfits and props?

Yes. You can discuss your outfit choices, color palettes, and styling requirements with us before the session. We also have private changing rooms available.

Do you provide photo editing and retouching?

Yes, professional editing is included in all packages. Selected photographs receive careful skin retouching, color grading, tone adjustments, and polish.

How long does a photoshoot take?

The duration typically ranges from 1 hour to a full day, depending on the chosen service, number of outfits, backdrops, and creative concepts.

Can I discuss a custom or creative photoshoot?

Absolutely! If you have a unique concept, theme, editorial moodboard, or commercial idea, simply contact us and we will design a tailored session for you.
